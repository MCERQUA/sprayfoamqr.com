DOMAIN CLASSIFICATION & PIVOT HISTORY
Not insurance, not a content-hub thought-leadership site. This is a real trust/credentialing
product: a public directory where spray foam contractors have a credentials profile page (license,
insurance status, certifications, photos), and CCA mails contractors a large QR sticker for their
work truck linking directly to that profile. A homeowner or GC scans the sticker in a driveway and
reads the contractor's credentials in ~10 seconds before letting a crew spray chemicals in an attic.

This build went through a mid-build pivot. It started as a QR-content-hub concept (thought-leadership
about QR codes in spray foam, mirroring the VR sibling site's pattern) with real research behind it
(Traciqo/QRWarranty/QRTRAC precedent, a documented 15%->65% warranty-registration case study,
EZQR/SuperCheapSigns QR-marketing precedent) — that research and the images generated for it carried
over, but Mike deprioritized/redirected the premise entirely to the real directory+profile product
described above. A written plan (route map, public/held field split, exact badge wording, sticker-
flow wiring) was sent to the manager for review before any scaffolding, revised once per 3 host
refinements (fulfillment-copy-only variability, VERIFICATION_ENABLED code-path gating, contractor-
asserted vs externally-checkable field tagging), approved, then built exactly as planned.
